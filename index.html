Fast capture after one real permission.

The hot path is ScreenCast + PipeWire. On portal v4+, the helper requests persistent permission and rotates the single-use restore token after every restored session. The one-shot Screenshot portal is recovery, not the steady-state architecture.

CAPTURE="$HOME/.agents/skills/gnome-wayland-computer-use/scripts/capture.sh"
"$CAPTURE" --timing --screen /tmp/screen.png

The first capture may ask you to choose a monitor. Later calls restore the approved source while permission remains valid.

Pixel-only means pixel-only.

A Vulkan or GLFW window can be plainly visible while exposing no useful AT-SPI tree and no semantic window record. The skill does not confuse that with absence. It captures the display, grounds visually, and acts by coordinates.

Installed web apps stay apps.

Standalone browser-backed apps keep their own identity through live window data, desktop IDs, StartupWMClass, --app-id=, and --app=. The resolver caches launcher inventory instead of rediscovering it every turn.

Route once → cheapest truthful evidence.

AX for readable state. Pixels for custom rendering. Deterministic click → type and type → submit spans stay together. Fresh evidence is paid for at real decision boundaries, not after every input.
