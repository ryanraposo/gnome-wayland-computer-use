why it is fast

Keep determined mechanics local.

action spans

Already-decided Cua actions share one MCP session instead of bouncing through the model.

postconditions

Wait for the fact that matters instead of repeatedly asking a screenshot what happened.

direct truth first

AT-SPI, filesystem, process, D-Bus and settings evidence beat pixels when they can answer cleanly.

durable truth

.gwcu remembers stable app identity, capabilities, calibration and preferences across sessions.

install

One installer. Then use the computer.

The installer qualifies Ubuntu 26.04 GNOME Wayland, prepares Cua and its GNOME helper, establishes compositor-approved control consent, installs the Agent Skill, and enables WORLDLINE plus the lazy visual observer.

curl -fsSL https://ryanraposo.github.io/gnome-wayland-computer-use/install.sh | bash
Run it as your logged-in desktop user. GNOME may show a “Remote Desktop” permission prompt for local EIS/libei control. Whole-screen observation has its own ScreenCast consent boundary.
