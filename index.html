Reliable ⚕ computer use ⚕ for Ubuntu 26.04 GNOME Wayland

How does it work? →
“Capture my desktop” Captures wallpaper and desktop icons through the compositor, without including covering app windows.
“Capture my screen” Captures the visible display, including windows, through the non-interactive Screenshot portal.
“Debug the app” A reliable computer-use foundation for GUI development: screenshots, accessibility-tree inspection, stable widget targeting, and verified clicks or text entry.
curl -fsSL https://ryanraposo.github.io/gnome-wayland-computer-use/install.sh | bash
First install: sign out of the GNOME session and sign back in once. This loads the desktop-capture extension and applies input-group membership if needed. Then start a new Hermes session. Until the GNOME session reload, the verified compatibility rung briefly runs Show Desktop → capture → restore.
